The wild-type (WT) APP coding sequence contains the normal nucleotide sequence at positions c.2010–2011, which corresponds to the codons encoding Lysine (Lys, K) at position 670 and Methionine (Met, M) at position 671. In the WT sequence, this region is part of the β-secretase cleavage site, which is critical for normal processing of the amyloid precursor protein. In the mutant sequence, the documented variant c.2010_2011inv (functionally equivalent to delinsTC) introduces a two-base inversion/substitution, changing the original nucleotides (AA) into (TC). This does not shift the reading frame, but it alters the codons at positions 670–671, producing a double amino acid substitution: Lys670→Asn and Met671→Leu (KM→NL). As a result, the protein length remains unchanged, but the local structure at the β-secretase cleavage site is altered, which increases cleavage efficiency and leads to increased amyloid-β (Aβ) production. This biochemical change is strongly associated with early-onset Alzheimer’s disease, due to enhanced plaque formation.
